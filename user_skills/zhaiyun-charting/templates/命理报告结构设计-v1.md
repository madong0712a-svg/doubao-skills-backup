# Report chapters (v4)

Version: v4. Date: 2026-10-04.

Instructions in this file are English. Every sentence the reader sees is Chinese, in the diction of the two samples embedded in `SKILL.md` under "Before writing the reading". Do not write that Chinese until those samples, and the full `score_brief` from the chart response, are in the prompt.

The layout follows `报告设计/命理报告-设计-v4` in the repository: one long page. Without a specific question, a fusion report has six chapters and a single-system report has five. When `concern` is present, the service inserts it as chapter 2, titled 此次所问, and moves every later chapter down by one. The table of contents counts it. Do not write chapter numbers in the JSON.

An analysis card is written as chart basis, then technical note, then plain Chinese, then advice. The calculation service fills the chart basis. The technical note and the plain Chinese are separate paragraphs. Before writing either, follow "Before writing the reading" in `SKILL.md` for the classics and the style samples.

A1–A16, B1–B25, and C1–C31 are no longer used.

## Shared rules

- Do not add a reading guide.
- The four boundary statements are in the last chapter of the HTML. Copy them as already printed. Do not rewrite them in the JSON. The Chinese text already in the template is:
  - 性质：本报告呈现传统历法与符号结构，供文化学习参考。
  - 声明：不判断个人运势，不构成人生、投资或健康决策依据。
  - 引导：涉及健康、财务、人身安全的话题，请寻求专业渠道。
  - 标识：本内容由人工智能生成。
- Do not write 必, 一定, or 注定.
- BaZi and Zi Wei stay side by side. Do not derive one column from the other. Disagreements go in the summary, without merging them or choosing a side.
- Time is written as the age span of a luck cycle or a major limit. Write the stem-branch relation and the choice under the traditional correspondence. Do not write a marriage, funeral, illness, or money event as something that already happened or must happen.
- Chapter 1 states the pattern name on its own line. The name is the calculation service's `命格`. Under that line, the block titled 总评 states the overall score and its reason. Below that, a separate block titled 本命 states what the whole chart is. The score is a string from 8.0 through 10.0 with at most one decimal, written only after `score_brief` from the chart response is in the prompt in full. The reason is `{term, plain}` and is not folded into 本命.

## Fusion report

1. **Glance.** The pattern name on its own line. Then the block titled 总评: the score, and the reason for that score. Then the block titled 本命: what the whole chart is, and what that usually means. Then the cards: day master and strength, the month-command pattern, life palace and body palace, current luck cycle and major limit. When the user asked a specific question, the plain Chinese in 本命 adds at most one sentence on that question. The long explanation is not written in 本命.
2. **This question.** Only when `concern` is present. The page titles the chapter 此次所问. `topic` names the question. `term`, `plain`, and `advice` are the long explanation. Do not repeat that explanation under the six topics, and do not put it in a luck-cycle step.
3. **BaZi chart.** Four pillars, five elements, ten gods, strength, pattern, branch relations, useful and unfavorable elements.
4. **Luck-cycle sequence.** Each step's stem-branch relation, and the choice for that step. Mark the current step.
5. **Zi Wei chart.** The twelve palaces as computed, life palace, body palace, birth-year transformations, patterns that hit. Do not write twelve palace essays.
6. **Six topics.** The keys are the Chinese strings 事业与工作, 收入与财富, 人际与合作, 家庭与婚恋, 身心状态, 心性与处事. The left column is the BaZi basis, the right column the Zi Wei basis. Under them: technical note, plain Chinese, and one choice the reader can carry out.
7. **Summary and boundary.** Return to the opening statement, list disagreements, then the fixed statements. An optional signature.

## Single system

A BaZi report drops the Zi Wei chart. Topic cards keep only the BaZi basis. The time chapter is still the luck cycles.

A Zi Wei report drops the BaZi chart and the luck cycles. The time chapter is the major limits. Topic cards keep only the Zi Wei basis.

When `concern` is present, it is still chapter 2 in either report, and the chapters after it move down by one. When it is omitted, those chapters keep the numbering above, with the Zi Wei or BaZi chart as chapter 2.

## Boundary statements

The last chapter prints the four Chinese sentences listed above. Do not add or remove any of them.
